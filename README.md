## Learning Path

### Foundations

- **[The English Master Course: English Grammar, English Speaking](https://www.udemy.com/course/the-english-master-course/)** — Strong academic English is non-negotiable for reading papers, writing, and presenting. Start here if English is not your first language.

- **[Productivity and Time Management for the Overwhelmed](https://www.udemy.com/course/productivity-and-time-management-for-the-overwhelmed/)** — Research is a long game. Building solid productivity habits early pays off throughout the journey.

- **[The Complete Python Developer](https://www.udemy.com/course/complete-python-developer-zero-to-mastery/)** — Python is the lingua franca of AI research. Get fluent before touching any ML framework.

- **[CS50's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/)** — Harvard's free intro to AI. Covers search, optimization, ML, neural networks, and NLP with hands-on projects. *Best free starting point for AI.*

- **[Data Structures + Algorithms](https://www.udemy.com/course/master-the-coding-interview-data-structures-algorithms/)** *(+ coding interviews prep)* — Essential for research engineering roles and ML systems work. Don't skip this.

- **[Mathematics for Machine Learning Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/mathematics-machine-learning)** *(Coursera – Imperial College London)* — Linear algebra, multivariate calculus, PCA. The mathematical backbone of everything in ML. *Do this in parallel with or before the ML specialization.*

### Core ML & Deep Learning

- **[Machine Learning Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/machine-learning-introduction)** *(Coursera – Andrew Ng)* — The gold standard intro to ML. Covers supervised/unsupervised learning, best practices, and modern techniques.
  - **[Google ML Engineer Path](https://cloud.google.com/learn/training/machinelearning-ai)** + **[Professional ML Engineer Certification](https://cloud.google.com/certification/machine-learning-engineer)** — Good follow-up to operationalize ML skills at scale.

- **[Deep Learning Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/deep-learning)** *(Coursera – Andrew Ng)* — Five-course deep dive into neural networks, CNNs, RNNs, transformers, and MLOps fundamentals. *The most referenced DL curriculum.*
  - Alternative: **[PyTorch for Deep Learning Bootcamp](https://www.udemy.com/course/pytorch-for-deep-learning-and-computer-vision/)** — More hands-on and code-first. Good complement if you prefer learning by building.

- **[NLP Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/natural-language-processing)** *(Coursera – deeplearning.ai)* — Covers everything from classic NLP to attention mechanisms and transformers. *Essential if your research touches language.*

- **[Anthropic Courses](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/get-started)** / **[OpenAI Academy](https://academy.openai.com/)** — Practical courses on working with modern LLMs, prompt engineering, and API usage. Useful for applied research and building prototypes.

### Research Skills

- **[Understanding Research Methods](https://www.coursera.org/learn/research-methods)** / **[Research Training from Elsevier Researcher Academy](https://researcheracademy.elsevier.com/)** — Learn how research actually works: hypothesis formulation, experimental design, writing, and publishing.

- **[CS197: AI Research Experiences](https://www.cs197.seas.harvard.edu/)** *(Harvard)* — Semester-long course on doing AI research. Covers paper reading, ideation, experimentation, and writing. *One of the few courses that teaches the craft of research directly.*

- **[Google DeepMind: AI Research Foundations](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/)** — Articles and resources from DeepMind on how to approach AI research problems.

- **[Understanding Deep Learning Research Tutorial – Theory, Code, and Math](https://github.com/Atcold/NYU-DLSP21)** *(NYU – Yann LeCun & Alfredo Canziani)* — A rigorous blend of theory and implementation. Great for bridging the gap between reading papers and understanding them deeply.

- **[How to Effectively Read Machine Learning Papers: A Beginner's Guide](https://towardsdatascience.com/how-to-read-a-paper-efficiently-under-3-hours-78dc1f77dade)** — Practical frameworks for getting the most out of papers quickly (three-pass method, etc.).

- **[Academic English Writing Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/academic-english)** *(Coursera – UC Davis)* — Writing clearly is a core research skill. Covers paragraphing, argumentation, and academic conventions.

- **[How to Avoid Machine Learning Pitfalls: A Guide for Academic Researchers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.02497)** *(Lones, 2021 – ArXiv)* — Practical checklist of common mistakes in ML experiments and papers. *Read this before writing your first paper.*

#### Additional resources

- **[The Illustrated Transformer](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/)** *(Jay Alammar)* — The best visual explanation of the Transformer architecture. Bookmark this.

- **[Andrej Karpathy's Neural Networks: Zero to Hero](https://karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html)** — From backprop from scratch to building GPT. Extremely practical and well-explained.

- **[Papers With Code](https://paperswithcode.com/)** — Every major ML paper linked to its implementation. Indispensable for replication and staying current.

- **[Connected Papers](https://www.connectedpapers.com/)** — Visual graph of related papers. Great for exploring a new research area.

- **[Distill.pub](https://distill.pub/)** — Interactive, beautifully written explainers of ML research concepts. High signal-to-noise ratio.

- **[ProfScout](https://profscout.com)** - Finds professors most likely to reply to research cold emails, ranked by reply-fit. Freemium.

## Access to GPU/TPU for Experiments

### Programs (Free Credits)

- **[AWS Cloud Credit for Research](https://aws.amazon.com/research-credits/)** — AWS offers cloud credits for academic researchers. Apply with a research proposal.

- **[NVIDIA Academic Grant Program](https://mynvidia.force.com/HardwareGrant/s/Application)** — Free GPU hardware grants for academic institutions. Useful for labs.

- **[Google TPU Research Cloud](https://sites.research.google/trc/about/)** / **[Learn JAX](https://jax.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)** — Free TPU access for researchers. JAX is the framework of choice at Google DeepMind. *This is a must.* 

- **[Google Colab Pro](https://colab.research.google.com/)** — Affordable GPU/TPU access with no setup. Good for prototyping and running medium-scale experiments.

- **[Kaggle Notebooks](https://www.kaggle.com/docs/notebooks)** — Free 30h/week GPU access. No setup, great for running experiments.

### Platforms (Pay-as-you-go)

- **[Vast.ai](https://vast.ai/)** — Marketplace for renting cheap GPUs from individual providers. Often 5–10× cheaper than AWS. *Best value for price.*

- **[RunPod](https://www.runpod.io/)** — Clean GPU cloud platform with good DX. Reliable and easy to spin up. Good alternative to Vast for slightly more stability.

- **[Lambda Labs](https://lambdalabs.com/)** — GPU cloud tailored for ML. Competitive pricing, good support for multi-GPU setups.

## Opportunities to Practice

### Research Communities

- **[Masakhane](https://www.masakhane.io/)** — Pan-African NLP and ML research community. Welcoming to newcomers, very active on GitHub and Discord. *Best entry point for African researchers into open research.* [GitHub](https://github.com/masakhane-io)

- **[CohereLabs Open Science](https://cohere.com/research)** — Cohere's open research arm. Runs the Aya project and other multilingual/low-resource NLP initiatives. Accepts external contributors.

- **[ML Collective](https://mlcollective.org/)** — Non-profit open research community. Connects independent researchers with mentors. Good if you want to do research outside academia.

- **[EleutherAI](https://www.eleuther.ai/)** — Grassroots AI research collective. Known for GPT-NeoX, The Pile, and other open-source LLM work. Very active Discord.

- **[HuggingFace Community](https://huggingface.co/)** — More than a platform — an active community around open ML. Contribute to models, datasets, and Spaces. Great visibility for your work.

- **[LAION](https://laion.ai/)** — Open non-profit behind LAION-5B and other large open datasets. Good community for those interested in large-scale data research.

### Hackathons & Competitions

- **[Zindi Africa](https://zindi.africa/)** — African data science and ML competition platform. Great way to build skills on real-world problems with African context.

- **[Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/)** — The largest ML competition platform. Wide range of problem types (CV, NLP, tabular). Building a track record here has real signal for research applications.

- **[HuggingFace Sprints](https://huggingface.co/blog/tag/community-sprint)** — Collaborative community events to build models, datasets, or demos in a few days. Low barrier to entry, high learning value.

- **[NeurIPS / ICML Competitions Track](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2024/CallForCompetitions)** — Research-grade competitions attached to top conferences. High effort but high recognition.

### Immersion Programs

- **[Residency Programs](https://github.com/dangkhoasdc/awesome-ai-residency)** — Structured 1-year research programs at companies like Google, Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI. *The fastest track into industry research for non-PhDs.*

- **[Google Student Researcher Program](https://research.google/careers/student-researcher/)** *(Bachelor, Master, PhD)* — Paid part-time research position at Google while enrolled in school. Highly competitive but extremely valuable.

- **[Cohere AI Scholars](https://cohere.com/blog/cohere-scholars)** — Research mentorship program for students interested in NLP. Cohort-based, with access to compute and researchers.

- **[Deep Learning Indaba](https://deeplearningindaba.com/)** — Annual week-long AI school and community gathering for African ML practitioners. *The most important African AI event of the year.*

- **[AMMI – African Master's in Machine Intelligence](https://aimsammi.org/)** — Master's program co-organized by Google DeepMind and Meta FAIR. Fully funded. *The top ML graduate program on the continent.*

- **[ML4D – Machine Learning for Developing World Workshop](https://ml4d.org/)** *(NeurIPS)* — Workshop focused on applying ML to global development challenges. Good entry point for research at the intersection of AI and impact.

### Entrepreneurship

- **[MEST AI Startup](https://meltwater.org/)** — Pan-African tech entrepreneurship program with funding and mentorship. Strong alumni network across West Africa.

- **[NVIDIA Inception](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/)** — Free program for AI startups. Provides technical support, cloud credits, and go-to-market resources.

- **[Google for Startups](https://startup.google.com/)** / **[AI Futures Fund](https://startup.google.com/programs/ai-futures-fund/)** — Equity-free funding and cloud credits for AI startups. The AI Futures Fund specifically targets startups building on top of AI.

- **[Microsoft for Startups (Founders Hub)](https://foundershub.startups.microsoft.com/)** — Up to $150k in Azure credits + GitHub Copilot and OpenAI API access. Low barrier to apply.

- **[Antler](https://www.antler.co/)** — Global early-stage VC with strong presence in Africa (Lagos, Nairobi). Co-builds startups from scratch, including AI companies.
